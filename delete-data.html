Delete Your TradeBook Data

Effective Date: October 8, 2026

This page explains how users of the TradeBook Android app can request deletion of some or all of their account-associated data without necessarily deleting their TradeBook account.

How to Request Data Deletion

To request deletion of your TradeBook data, send an email to:

Email: adityag246@gmail.com

Please use the email address associated with your TradeBook account and include the subject: TradeBook Data Deletion Request

In your request, clearly specify which data you want to have deleted. We may request sufficient information to verify that the request is associated with the account owner before processing the request.

Types of Data That May Be Deleted

Depending on the user's request and the data associated with the account, the following information may be deleted:

  • Individual trading records.
  • Trading notes and journal information.
  • Account-associated balance and journal data.
  • Other account-associated data that can be deleted without deleting the entire account.

Data That May Be Kept

Some information may need to be retained when required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, financial, or technical reasons. Such information will be retained only for as long as necessary for the applicable purpose.

Data stored only locally on your device may remain on the device until the TradeBook app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled.

Backup Data

If you have exported or created a TradeBook backup, copies of that backup may remain in locations where you saved them. Users are responsible for deleting exported backup files from their own device, computer, cloud storage, or other storage location.

Account Deletion

This page is for requesting deletion of selected data without necessarily deleting the entire TradeBook account. If you want to delete your complete TradeBook account and associated account data, please use the separate account deletion process provided by TradeBook.

Processing Time

Data deletion requests will be reviewed and processed within a reasonable period after the request has been verified.
