Delete Your TradeBook Account

Effective Date: October 8, 2026

This page explains how users of the TradeBook Android app can request deletion of their account and associated data.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your TradeBook account, send an email to:

Email: adityag246@gmail.com

Please send the request from the email address associated with your TradeBook account and include the subject: TradeBook Account Deletion Request

We may request sufficient information to verify that the request is associated with the account owner before processing the deletion request.

What Data Will Be Deleted

When an account deletion request is verified and processed, the following account-associated data will be deleted where applicable:

  • TradeBook account information associated with the account.
  • Trading records associated with the account, where stored with the account.
  • Account-associated balance and journal data stored by TradeBook.
  • Other account-associated data that is not required to be retained by law or for legitimate security purposes.

Data That May Be Retained

Some information may need to be retained for a limited period when required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, financial, or technical reasons. Such information will be retained only for as long as necessary for the applicable purpose.

Data stored only locally on your device may also remain on the device until the TradeBook app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled.

Backup Data

If you have created a TradeBook backup, you are responsible for deleting any exported backup files that you have saved on your device, computer, cloud storage, or other location.

Premium Subscription

Deleting your TradeBook account does not automatically guarantee cancellation of an active Google Play subscription. If you have an active subscription, manage or cancel it through your Google Play account.

Processing Time

Account deletion requests will be reviewed and processed within a reasonable period after the request has been verified.
