Privacy Policy – TradeBook

Effective Date: 8 October 2026

TradeBook ("we", "our", or "the App") is a trading journal and trade-management application designed to help users record, organize, review, and analyze their trading activity.

This Privacy Policy explains how TradeBook handles information when you use the application.

1. Information We Collect

TradeBook may process information that you choose to enter into the App, including:

  • Trade details and trading records
  • Trading instrument or symbol
  • Buy/Sell information
  • Trade quantity and price
  • Profit and loss information
  • Balance-related information
  • Notes and other information entered by you
  • Analytics and journal information generated from your records
  • Backup data created through the App

TradeBook does not require your real name, home address, phone number, or similar personal identification information for the basic trading-journal features.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information may be used to:

  • Record and manage trades
  • Calculate trading statistics
  • Display analytics and performance information
  • Maintain your trading journal
  • Provide balance and trade-history information
  • Provide analysis or Coach features
  • Create and restore backups
  • Improve the functionality and reliability of the App

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Local Storage

TradeBook may store trade records and related information locally on your device. Your locally stored trading information is intended to remain under your control.

If you uninstall the App or clear its application data, locally stored information may be deleted unless you have previously created a backup.

4. Backup and Restore

TradeBook may provide backup and restore functionality. Backup files may contain information stored in the App.

You are responsible for keeping backup files secure and should not share them with people you do not trust.

5. Internet and Third-Party Services

Some TradeBook features may require an internet connection. Third-party services may process limited technical or account-related information when necessary to provide those features.

TradeBook does not sell your trading records or personal information to third parties.

6. Google Play Billing

TradeBook may offer optional premium features through Google Play. Purchases and subscriptions are processed through Google Play Billing.

TradeBook does not directly collect or store your complete payment-card information. Google may process payment and subscription information according to Google's applicable terms and privacy policy.

7. Advertising

TradeBook does not sell your personal information for advertising purposes.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers when reasonably necessary to provide App functionality, process subscriptions, maintain security, diagnose technical problems, or comply with applicable legal requirements.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed by TradeBook. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored information remains on your device until you delete it or remove the App data.

Backup files created by you may remain wherever you have saved or shared them. You are responsible for deleting those files when they are no longer required.

11. Children's Privacy

TradeBook is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for the purpose of creating user profiles or accounts.

12. Permissions

TradeBook may request device permissions when they are required for specific functionality, such as creating, importing, exporting, or restoring files.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when TradeBook's features, services, or applicable legal requirements change.

The updated policy will be made available through this webpage.
